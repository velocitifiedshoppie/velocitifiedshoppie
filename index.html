Questions

How long until my order starts?

Orders begin after we confirm your payment. Smaller packs usually start sooner than very large ones. You can check progress any time under "Track your order" below.

Do you need my password?

No. We never ask for your password. We only need your public profile link or reel/post link.

Why must my account be public?

We can only deliver to public accounts. If views and likes are hidden, or "Flagged for review" is on, the numbers will not increase.

Can followers drop?

Followers can drop over time. Low drop packs cost more because they are meant to drop less. If something looks wrong, use the help form below.

How do I pay?

Scan the QR code or use the UPI ID on the payment page. Then type the exact amount you paid and your UTR / transaction ID.

Can I use the free demo again?

The free demo is one per customer, and it must be ordered on its own.
